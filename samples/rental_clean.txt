RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made and executed at Bengaluru on this 1st day of March, 2026, by and between Mr. Ramesh Kumar, son of Late Shri Gopal Kumar, aged about 58 years, residing at No. 14, 3rd Cross, Jayanagar, Bengaluru 560011 (hereinafter referred to as the "Landlord") of the One Part;

AND

Ms. Priya Sharma, daughter of Shri Anil Sharma, aged about 27 years, having her permanent address at 22 Civil Lines, Jaipur 302006 (hereinafter referred to as the "Tenant") of the Other Part.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the absolute owner of the residential flat bearing No. 304, Lakeview Apartments, 8th Main, BTM Layout, Bengaluru 560076 (hereinafter called the "Premises"), and the Tenant has approached the Landlord to take the Premises on rent for residential purposes, the parties agree as follows:

1. TERM
1.1 The tenancy shall be for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st March, 2026 and ending on 31st January, 2027.
1.2 The tenancy may be renewed for a further period by mutual written consent of both parties, with a rent increase not exceeding 5% of the last paid rent.

2. RENT
2.1 The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty Five Thousand only) to the Landlord on or before the 5th day of every English calendar month.
2.2 Rent shall be paid by bank transfer to the account designated by the Landlord in writing.
2.3 If the rent is not paid by the 10th day of the month, the Tenant shall pay a late fee of Rs. 500/- for each week of delay.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 The Tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 1,00,000/- (Rupees One Lakh only) to the Landlord, the receipt of which the Landlord hereby acknowledges.
3.2 The security deposit shall be refunded to the Tenant within 15 days of the Tenant vacating the Premises, after deducting any unpaid rent, unpaid utility bills and the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear.

4. MAINTENANCE AND UTILITIES
4.1 The Tenant shall pay the electricity and water charges as per the actual meter readings directly to the concerned authorities.
4.2 The monthly society maintenance charge of Rs. 2,500/- shall be paid by the Landlord.
4.3 Minor day-to-day repairs such as replacing bulbs and tap washers shall be done by the Tenant at her own cost. Major structural repairs, plumbing and electrical faults not caused by the Tenant shall be the responsibility of the Landlord.

5. USE OF PREMISES
5.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises for residential purposes only and shall not carry on any commercial activity.
5.2 The Tenant shall not sublet, assign or part with possession of the Premises or any part thereof without the prior written consent of the Landlord.
5.3 The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations to the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

6. LOCK-IN PERIOD
6.1 Both parties agree to a lock-in period of 3 (three) months from the commencement date. During the lock-in period neither party may terminate this agreement except for breach.

7. TERMINATION AND NOTICE
7.1 After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement by giving one month's prior written notice to the other party.
7.2 The Landlord may terminate this agreement if the Tenant fails to pay rent for two consecutive months, after giving the Tenant 15 days' written notice to pay the arrears.
7.3 On termination or expiry, the Tenant shall hand over vacant and peaceful possession of the Premises to the Landlord in the same condition as received, subject to normal wear and tear.

8. INSPECTION
8.1 The Landlord or his authorised representative may inspect the Premises at a reasonable time after giving at least 24 hours' notice to the Tenant.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
9.1 This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of this agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Bengaluru.

10. STAMP DUTY
10.1 The stamp duty and registration charges for this agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this agreement on the date first written above.
