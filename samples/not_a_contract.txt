Weekend Masala Dosa Recipe

Soak two cups of rice and half a cup of urad dal for six hours. Grind into a smooth batter and let it ferment overnight in a warm place.

For the potato filling, boil four potatoes, then saute mustard seeds, curry leaves, green chillies and onions. Add turmeric and the mashed potatoes, and season with salt.

Heat a tawa, spread a ladle of batter thinly, drizzle oil, and cook until golden. Place the filling in the centre, fold, and serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.

Tip: a cast-iron tawa gives the crispiest dosas. Enjoy with family on a Sunday morning!
