RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Mysuru on 20th March, 2026 between Mrs. Lakshmi Narayan, residing at 12, Saraswathipuram, Mysuru (hereinafter referred to as the "Landlord") and Mr. Rohit Desai, residing at 45, Kuvempunagar, Mysuru (hereinafter referred to as the "Tenant").

1. TERM
1.1 The tenancy shall be for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st April, 2026 and ending on 31st December, 2026.

2. RENT
2.1 The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 20,000/- (Rupees Twenty Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of each month.
2.2 If rent is not paid by the 10th day of the month, the Tenant shall pay a late fee of Rs. 300/-.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 The Tenant has paid an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 60,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand only).
3.2 The security deposit shall be refunded within 30 days of the Tenant vacating the premises, after deducting unpaid dues.

4. LOCK-IN
4.1 Both parties agree to a lock-in period of 12 (twelve) months from the commencement date, during which neither party may terminate this agreement.

5. TERMINATION
5.1 After the lock-in period, the Tenant may terminate this agreement by giving one month's written notice to the Landlord.
5.2 The Tenant may terminate this agreement at any time by giving two months' written notice to the Landlord.
5.3 The Landlord may terminate this agreement by giving one month's written notice to the Tenant.

6. MAINTENANCE
6.1 Minor repairs shall be carried out by the Tenant and major repairs by the Landlord.

7. GOVERNING LAW
7.1 This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Mysuru shall have jurisdiction.

SCHEDULE A - PAYMENT SUMMARY
Monthly rent: The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 22,000/- as per this schedule.
Security deposit: Rs. 60,000/-
