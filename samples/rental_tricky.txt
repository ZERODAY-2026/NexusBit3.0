LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Leave and License Agreement is entered into at Pune on 15th April, 2026 between M/s. Skyline Estates Private Limited, having its registered office at Baner Road, Pune 411045 (hereinafter called the "Licensor") and Mr. Arjun Mehta, aged 31 years, residing at Flat 9B, Green Park, Pune (hereinafter called the "Licensee").

1. GRANT OF LICENSE
1.1 The Licensor grants to the Licensee a license to occupy Flat No. 702, Skyline Towers, Baner, Pune for a period of 11 months from 1st May, 2026.

2. LICENSE FEE
2.1 The Licensee shall pay a monthly license fee of Rs. 32,000/- in advance on or before the 1st day of each month.
2.2 The Licensor may revise the license fee from time to time at its sole discretion, and the revised fee shall be payable from the date communicated by the Licensor.
2.3 Any delay in payment shall attract interest at 24% per annum along with a penalty of Rs. 1,000/- per day of delay.

3. DEPOSIT
3.1 The Licensee shall pay an interest-free refundable deposit of Rs. 3,00,000/- (Rupees Three Lakhs only).
3.2 The Licensor may deduct from the deposit such amounts as it deems fit towards painting, cleaning, repairs and any other charges, and the decision of the Licensor in this regard shall be final.
3.3 If the Licensee vacates the premises before the expiry of 11 months for any reason whatsoever, the entire deposit shall be forfeited and shall not be refunded.

4. MAINTENANCE
4.1 All repairs, whether minor or major, including structural repairs, shall be carried out by the Licensee at his own cost.
4.2 The Licensee shall keep the premises in a satisfactory condition.

5. TERMINATION
5.1 The Licensor may terminate this license at any time without assigning any reason by giving 7 days' notice to the Licensee.
5.2 The Licensee may terminate this license only after the expiry of the lock-in period of 11 months, by giving 3 months' notice to the Licensor.
5.3 Upon termination the Licensee shall vacate the premises promptly, failing which the Licensor may remove the belongings of the Licensee without any notice.

6. ENTRY
6.1 The Licensor and its agents shall be entitled to enter the premises at any time for inspection or for showing the premises to prospective licensees.

7. GENERAL
7.1 The Licensee shall comply with all rules framed by the Licensor from time to time.
7.2 The Licensee waives all rights to raise any claim against the Licensor for any loss or damage to his belongings, howsoever caused.
7.3 The Licensee shall indemnify the Licensor against all losses, costs and damages arising out of the Licensee's use of the premises.

8. DISPUTES
8.1 Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Licensor, whose decision shall be final and binding.

Signed by the Licensor: For Skyline Estates Pvt. Ltd., Authorised Signatory
Signed by the Licensee: Arjun Mehta
